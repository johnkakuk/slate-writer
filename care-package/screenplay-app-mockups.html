INT. ELI'S APARTMENT — NIGHT
Eli digs through a junk drawer. Batteries, dead pens, a broken watch. His hand stops on something else — an envelope, unopened, addressed in handwriting he knows too well.
INT. ELI'S APARTMENT — CONTINUOUS
He dials. It rings out. The beep comes too fast.
ELI
Hey, it's — obviously it's me. Call me back. Please.
INT. DINER — NIGHT
Rain streaks the window. MARA (30s, exhausted) turns a coffee cup in slow circles. Across from her, ELI watches, waiting.
ELI
You're not going to say anything?
MARA
What's left to say.
EXT. DINER PARKING LOT — CONTINUOUS
Rain picks up. Mara stands by the passenger door, arms crossed. Eli waits at the driver's side, keys in hand.
MARA
I'm not getting in that car until you tell me the truth.
INT. CAR — HIGHWAY — NIGHT
Twelve miles of silence. Headlights sweep the dashboard. Finally, Eli speaks without looking over.
ELI
It wasn't supposed to happen like this.
